Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Tickwell keeps your tasks on your phone. Signing in is optional: if you sign in, your backup is stored in your own account so you can restore it on a new phone. We don’t sell your data, and there are no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

This policy explains what happens to your information when you use Tickwell: To-Do List & Planner (“Tickwell”), an Android app made by Horyaal Tech (“we”, “us”).

What Tickwell stores on your phone

Everything you add to Tickwell is saved only on your phone, including:

  • your tasks, subtasks, notes, dates, reminders, priorities and categories
  • your habits and their history, focus sessions, points and streaks
  • your name and profile photo, if you choose to add them
  • photos you choose as backgrounds
  • your settings

Home screen widgets keep a copy of your task titles and times on your phone so they can show them. If you don’t sign in, none of this leaves your phone.

Smart Scan

When you use Smart Scan, the text in your photo or screenshot is read on your phone. Your photo is not uploaded anywhere.

The text-reading files are built into the app. If they can’t be loaded, Tickwell may download the same standard files from a public content delivery network (jsDelivr). That download doesn’t include your photo or any of your tasks, but like any internet request, the network can see your device’s IP address.

Accounts and cloud backup (optional)

You can use every feature of Tickwell without an account. If you choose to sign in with Google or with an email and password, we use Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore) to run your account and keep your backup.

When you sign in, we store:

  • Account details: your email address, an account ID, and, if you sign in with Google, the name and profile picture link Google shares with us.
  • Your backup, when you tap Back up now or turn on automatic backup: your tasks, habits and their history, categories, settings, profile name, focus history, points and streaks, plus when the backup was made and how many tasks it holds.

Your backup does not include your profile photo, your background photos, or your Tickwell Pro status. Each new backup replaces the previous one.

The backup is stored on Google’s servers in the United States. It is encrypted while it travels and while it is stored, and our security rules only let your own signed-in account read or change it. It is not end-to-end encrypted, which means it is technically possible for Horyaal Tech to access it. We don’t look at your backup unless you ask us to help with a problem, or the law requires it.

We keep your account and backup until you delete them. Google processes this data for us under its Firebase terms, and doesn’t use it for advertising.

Permissions

  • Notifications: to show your task reminders and the optional morning and evening summaries. These are created on your phone.
  • Alarms & reminders: only if you allow it, so reminders can arrive at the exact minute.
  • Photos and camera: Tickwell uses Android’s photo picker and your camera app only when you choose to add a photo. It doesn’t get access to your whole photo library.
  • Internet: to open links such as this page, to open your email app when you send feedback, for the Smart Scan download described above, and, if you sign in, to reach your account and backup.

Things you choose to share

  • Export Backup creates a file with your tasks and settings. You choose where it goes using Android’s share menu. After you share it, the app or service you picked handles it under its own policy.
  • Emails to us: if you email support@horyaaltech.com, we receive your email address and your message. We use them only to reply to you and delete them when they’re no longer needed.

Tickwell Pro purchases

Tickwell Pro subscriptions are sold through Google Play. Google handles the payment, and we never see your card or payment details. Google tells the app whether you have an active subscription so Pro features can be unlocked. Google’s own privacy policy applies to purchases.

No ads, analytics or tracking

Tickwell doesn’t show ads and doesn’t contain advertising or analytics tools. We don’t sell personal data or share it with anyone except Google, which runs sign-in, backups and purchases for us.

Deleting your data

Data on your phone is in your control. You can delete tasks in the app, use Settings > Data > Clear Completed Tasks, or remove everything by uninstalling Tickwell or clearing its storage in your phone’s settings. We can’t recover deleted data.

To delete your account and your cloud backup, open Tickwell, tap Menu, then your name at the top, then Delete account. This deletes them straight away. If you no longer have the app, see how to delete your account. Signing out doesn’t delete anything.

Children

Tickwell is not aimed at children under 13.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have rights over your personal data, including the right to see, correct, export and delete it. If you don’t sign in, we don’t hold your app data. If you do, you can restore or delete your backup at any time in the app, or email us for a copy of what we hold. If you have emailed us, you can ask for a copy of that correspondence or ask us to delete it. If you’re unhappy with how we’ve handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.

Changes to this policy

If we change how Tickwell handles your information, we’ll update this policy before the change is released and change the date at the top of this page.
