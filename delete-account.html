Delete your Tickwell account

Tickwell: To-Do List & Planner, by Horyaal Tech

Deleting your account removes your account and your cloud backup. Tasks saved on your phone are not affected.

Delete it in the app (fastest)

  1. Open Tickwell and tap Menu.
  2. Tap your name at the top. This opens Account & backup.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm. You may be asked to sign in or enter your password again.

Your account and backup are deleted straight away.

Ask us to delete it

If you no longer have the app, email support@horyaaltech.com from the email address you used for Tickwell, with the subject “Delete my Tickwell account”. We’ll delete your account and backup within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your account: email address, account ID, and the name and picture link from Google if you used Google sign-in.
  • Your cloud backup: tasks, habits and their history, categories, settings, focus history, points and streaks.

Nothing is kept afterwards, except the emails you sent us, which we keep only until your request is finished. Tasks stored on your phone stay there until you uninstall Tickwell or clear its storage.
